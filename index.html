Pecha PDF Alignment

Upload your PDF and arrange consecutive pages into balanced duplex parts.

Choose Alignment Parts Consecutive blocks per physical sheet

Duplex Alignment Rule: Pages are divided into consecutive parts while keeping duplex page-pairs (1/2, 3/4, 5/6...) together. Front positions are normal (left-to-right) and back positions are reversed (right-to-left). Automatic blank positions are added only where a physical sheet slot is unused. All operations run 100% locally in your browser.

Tools / Printing Layout

Printing Layout

Arrange complete Pecha PDF pages vertically on printable sheets with zero margin and zero gap.

Choose Paper Size Standard sheet dimensions

Printing Specifications: Pages are placed vertically with 0 mm margin and 0 mm gap. The complete group of pages is centered on every sheet. The output PDF embeds vector streams directly without rasterization or JPEG recompression. A 0.15 mm cutting stroke is added at output sheet positions 1, 51, 101, 151, 201, and so on. If a page exceeds sheet dimensions, an alert asks whether to proportionally fit it.
